# Training: Bar and kitchen

**Time:** 1 hour · **Sign in as:** 1007 Ishara (bartender, PIN 7777) or 1005 Suresh (kitchen, PIN 5555)

## What you will be able to do

- Read kitchen and bar tickets (KOT/BOT), including modifiers, notes, seats, allergens and voids
- Mark dishes out of stock (86) and back on sale
- (Bartenders) take bar orders and payments

## 1. Kitchen and bar tickets (20 min)

When a steward taps **Send**, each item prints at its station: food at the kitchen printer, drinks at the bar printer.

A ticket shows:

- the check number, table, order type, steward and time;
- each item in large type with its quantity;
- `>` modifiers (for example `> Extra spicy`) and `NOTE:` the guest's request;
- `Seat n` when seats are used;
- **CONTAINS: …** in reverse print when the items contain allergens: take extra care;
- `VOID 1 x …` on a void ticket: stop preparing that item;
- `*** REPRINT ***` on a reprinted ticket: check it isn't already made.

**Exercise 1.** The trainer sends an order with modifiers, a note and an allergen. Read the ticket back aloud.

**Exercise 2.** The trainer voids one item. Find the void ticket and say what to do.

## 2. 86: out of stock (15 min)

When a dish runs out:

1. Sign in on any terminal or phone.
2. **Functions** (phone: **More → Functions**) → **Item availability (86)**.
3. Tap the dish. It shows **86** and becomes *Sold out* on every device immediately.
4. When it is available again, tap it once more.

**Exercise 3.** Mark Lamprais 86, check on a phone that it shows *Sold out*, then put it back on sale.

## 3. Bartenders: orders and payment (25 min)

Bartenders work like cashiers in the **Bar** revenue centre. Follow sections 2 and 3 of the [cashier manual](02-cashier.md): take the drinks order, send it, and take payment.
